SEVEN DAYS · ONE HOME

A Week in Hirakata

The Perfect Base Between Osaka and Kyoto

Some trips are about checking famous places off a list.

This one is different.

Stay in one place, unpack only once, and spend a week exploring Japan at your own pace.

Located between Kyoto and Osaka, The Showa House is more than just a place to sleep. It is a home where you can experience everyday life in Japan while making easy day trips across Kansai.

Here is what one memorable week could look like.

Day 1

Arrive in Hirakata

Arrive at Kuzuha Station, your gateway to a week in Japan.

After checking in, take a walk through the quiet neighborhood. Stop by a local supermarket or convenience store and pick up an onigiri or something simple for breakfast the next morning.

There is no need to rush anywhere today.

Settle into the house, unpack your bags, and enjoy your first evening in an ordinary Japanese neighborhood.

Day 2

Kyoto

Start the morning with an onigiri from the local convenience store before taking the train to Kyoto.

Walk through the thousands of vermilion torii gates at Fushimi Inari Shrine, explore the historic streets around Kiyomizu-dera, enjoy matcha sweets, and finish the day in Gion.

Then return, not to another hotel, but to the same comfortable home.

Day 3

Osaka

Today is all about Osaka’s energy.

Explore the lively streets of Namba, and in the evening head to Dotonbori.

Take a photo beneath the famous Glico sign and enjoy takoyaki or kushikatsu.

Dotonbori, filled with neon lights and crowds, has an atmosphere unlike anywhere else in Japan. The number of people may surprise you at first, but that is part of Osaka’s charm.

Experience this lively, energetic and slightly unusual city for yourself.

Day 4

A Slower Day at Iwashimizu Hachimangu

After two busy cities, it is time to slow down.

Just a short trip from The Showa House, Iwashimizu Hachimangu Shrine offers a quieter side of Japan that many overseas visitors never discover.

Walk up the wooded approach rather than taking the cable car, visit the shrine, and enjoy the view from the observation area.

Afterwards, stop at a café and spend an unhurried afternoon nearby.

Official website: Iwashimizu Hachimangu Shrine ↗

Day 5

Nara

Spend the day in Nara, where deer wander freely through the park.

Wander through Nara Park, and take your time enjoying its open lawns, wooded paths and slower rhythm.

If you would like to see more, Todai-ji Temple is an easy addition to the day.

If you still have energy, continue to Kasuga Taisha Shrine.

Compared with Kyoto and Osaka, Nara feels a little more relaxed.

Day 6

Uji

One more day is a luxury.

Spend it in Uji, a peaceful town known for green tea and the beautiful Byodo-in Temple.

Enjoy authentic matcha, walk beside the river, and take your time.

You could also choose Arashiyama, Kobe, or return to a favorite place from earlier in the week.

The important thing is that you do not have to rush.

Day 7

Home

Pack your bags slowly.

Open the shoji one last time and let the morning light into the room.

A week ago, this was simply the place where you were staying.

Now, it may feel a little like home.

Many guests tell us the same thing before they leave:

“I wish I had stayed longer.”
