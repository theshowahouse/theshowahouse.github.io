Access

One Peaceful Home Base for Kyoto, Osaka, Nara, and Uji

Spend your days exploring, then return to the calm of a quiet residential neighborhood and the slower rhythm of everyday Japan.

ARRIVAL

Getting Here

10

Minute walk

The Showa House is approximately a 10-minute walk from Kuzuha Station through a typical residential neighborhood, along mostly flat streets. A taxi stand is available at the station for guests arriving with luggage or who prefer not to walk.

Keihan Main Line

Kuzuha is served by the Keihan Main Line, making day trips toward both central Kyoto and Osaka straightforward.

↗

Arriving with luggage

The walk is generally manageable with wheeled luggage. As with many residential areas in Japan, surfaces and street conditions may vary along the way.

The Showa House gives you one peaceful home base for days in Kyoto, Osaka, Nara, and Uji. Explore each place at your own pace, then come home to a neighborhood where everyday Japanese life continues at a gentler rhythm.
