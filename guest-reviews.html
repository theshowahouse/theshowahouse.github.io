What Our Guests Say About The Showa House

Guests from around the world have stayed at The Showa House and shared their experiences. Again and again, they mention the quiet neighborhood, the traditional Japanese atmosphere, easy access to Kyoto and Osaka, and the personal hospitality of our family.

THE EXPERIENCE

What Guests Love About The Showa House

01

A Peaceful Base Between Kyoto and Osaka

Guests often describe The Showa House as a peaceful place to return to after busy days in Kyoto or Osaka. Kuzuha Station also makes it easy to explore Kyoto, Osaka, Uji, Nara and other parts of Kansai.

02

Experience Everyday Japan

Many guests say that staying here feels very different from staying in a hotel or in the middle of a tourist district. The Showa House offers a chance to experience a quieter, more everyday side of Japan.

03

Stay in a Traditional Japanese Home

Tatami rooms, futons, traditional details and the atmosphere of an older Japanese house are an important part of the experience. Guests often describe the house as unique, nostalgic, authentic and memorable.

04

Personal Hospitality

Guests frequently mention conversations with the host family, local recommendations, station pick-ups and small acts of hospitality as some of their favorite memories of their stay.
