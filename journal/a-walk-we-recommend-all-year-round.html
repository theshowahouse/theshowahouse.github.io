A Walk We Recommend All Year Round

One of our favorite places near Kyoto, beautiful in every season.

Most visitors know Sewaritei for its spectacular cherry blossoms.

And they're absolutely right.

Every spring, hundreds of cherry trees bloom along the Yodo River, creating one of the most beautiful riverside walks near Kyoto.

But that's not the only reason we recommend this place.

What we love most is the feeling of space.

Wide open skies, peaceful riverside paths, and a relaxed atmosphere make Sewaritei a wonderful place to visit throughout the year.

In spring, the blossoms create a breathtaking tunnel of pink.

During summer, fresh greenery and cool river breezes make it a pleasant place for a walk.

Autumn brings quieter scenery, while winter offers peaceful views with very few visitors.

It isn't just a place to see cherry blossoms.

It's a place where local people come to slow down, walk, enjoy a picnic, or simply spend time outdoors.

If you have time after your walk, we also recommend visiting Iwashimizu Hachimangu Shrine, located on nearby Mount Otokoyama.

The quiet forest paths and historic shrine provide a completely different atmosphere, making the two destinations a perfect half-day trip.

If you're staying at The Showa House for several nights, this is one of the places we'd happily recommend—not only during cherry blossom season, but throughout the year.

Sometimes the best travel memories come from places where local people simply enjoy spending time.

Visitor Information
