Where to Buy Bento & Groceries Near The Showa House

Bento, groceries, and easy takeaway meals around Kuzuha

You may not want to eat at a restaurant every night during your trip.

After a long day exploring Kyoto or Osaka, picking up a bento or some prepared dishes and enjoying a quiet dinner at The Showa House can be a relaxing option.

Around Kuzuha Station, you'll find several excellent places to buy bentos, prepared foods, groceries, drinks, and other takeaway meals.

Our Favourite Supermarkets

There are several supermarkets and food stores near The Showa House where you can easily buy bento boxes, groceries, drinks, and prepared foods.

Each offers a different experience, so you can choose the one that best suits your trip.

  • AEON Food Style
    Best all-round supermarket
  • Top World
    Neighbourhood favourite
  • Keihan Department Store
    Premium food hall

AEON Food Style

Best for: One-stop shopping

AEON Food Style is the largest supermarket near Kuzuha Station and the easiest place to shop during your stay.

You'll find bento boxes, sushi, fresh food, drinks, snacks, toiletries, and almost everything you might need.

Its convenient location inside KUZUHA MALL and longer opening hours make it our first recommendation when you simply want one place that has everything.

Top World

Best for: Local experience

Top World is a neighbourhood supermarket that many local residents use every day. It is especially popular for its excellent bento boxes and takeaway sushi.

If you want to try supermarket food that many local residents genuinely enjoy, this is one of our favourite places.

Compared with larger supermarkets, it feels more personal and local. If you'd like to experience everyday life in Japan, this is an excellent choice.

📍 View on Google Maps ↗

Keihan Department Store

Best for: Premium bento & deli foods

Keihan Department Store offers the highest-quality selection of prepared foods in the area.

On the first floor, you'll find excellent bento boxes, fresh sushi, Japanese sweets, Western-style desserts, and many other foods.

The specialty food section also includes delicatessen shops offering freshly prepared dishes.

If you're looking for beautifully prepared meals, premium bento boxes, or high-quality Japanese food, this is our top recommendation.

📍 View on Google Maps ↗

Takeout Shops

If you would rather choose food from a shop specialising in bento boxes and prepared dishes, these are our recommendations.

  • Karaichi
    Japanese fried chicken
  • Sakana
    Handmade bento & prepared foods

Karaichi (Japanese Fried Chicken)

Takeout only (no dine-in)

Karaichi is a takeout-only fried chicken shop near The Showa House.

Our recommendation is the Mixed Bento, which includes both juicy chicken thigh and lean chicken breast.

It is a convenient choice when you want to bring a warm and filling dinner back to the house.

📍 View on Google Maps ↗

Sakana (Prepared Foods)

Sakana (Japanese: 咲菜) is a prepared-food shop located inside Kuzuha Station.

It offers a wide selection of handmade side dishes as well as excellent bento boxes, making it one of our favourite places for an easy dinner.

Its location inside the station is especially convenient when returning from a day trip, allowing you to pick up dinner before walking back to The Showa House.

📍 View on Google Maps ↗

Convenience Stores

Japanese convenience stores are useful when you need something quickly or late at night.

Around Kuzuha Station and The Showa House, you can find stores such as 7-Eleven, Lawson, and FamilyMart.

They sell rice balls, sandwiches, salads, desserts, drinks, snacks, and basic travel necessities.

A Small Travel Tip

We recommend visiting a Japanese supermarket at least once during your stay.

A supermarket is not a tourist attraction, but it is a place where local people shop every day.

Looking through the bento boxes, prepared dishes, snacks, and drinks is a simple way to experience a little of everyday life in Japan.

Bringing dinner back and enjoying it slowly at The Showa House can also be a pleasant way to end a busy day.
