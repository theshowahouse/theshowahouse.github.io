Even Fast Food Feels Different in Japan

Familiar names, different flavours—and three easy choices around Kuzuha Station

From Taka’s Notebook

When I travel abroad, I do not usually look for Japanese restaurants. I want to eat what local people eat in their everyday lives—pho and bánh mì in Vietnam, or lu rou fan and noodles in Taiwan. For me, that is one of the pleasures of travelling.

So I used to imagine that visitors to Japan would want Japanese food at every meal.

But many guests also go to McDonald’s or KFC. Sometimes they want a familiar taste. Sometimes they simply want an easy meal in a place they already know. Often, they seem to go without thinking very much about it.

Then they come back and tell me, “It tastes completely different from the KFC in my country,” or, “The service is so good here.”

They enter looking for something familiar, but end up discovering another side of Japan.

Food does not have to be traditional to become part of a travel experience. Differences can be found in the flavour, the menu, the way the food is presented and the care shown by the staff. Even fast food can tell you something about a country.

A familiar restaurant can become an unexpected part of your Japan experience.

At a glance: all three are by Kuzuha Station

MOS Burger is in the station building. McDonald’s and KFC are in Kuzuha Mall beside the station. The station area is about a 10-minute walk from The Showa House.

Three easy choices near Kuzuha Station

All three restaurants below are around Kuzuha Station, which is about a 10-minute walk from The Showa House. Opening hours and menus may change, so please check the linked store information before you visit.

A familiar name, the Japanese version

McDonald’s — Kuzuha Mall

The golden arches may be familiar, but the experience may not be quite the same. Look for seasonal items and menu choices you may not have at home, and notice the presentation, atmosphere and service.

Location: Kuzuha Mall, a short walk from Kuzuha Station

The taste guests often compare with home

KFC — Kuzuha Mall

Several guests have told us that Japanese KFC tastes very different from the KFC they know at home. The seasoning, texture and menu may not be what you expect. Some guests are just as surprised by the polite, attentive service.

Location: South Building restaurant area, 1F, Kuzuha Mall

Fast food born in Japan

MOS Burger — Kuzuha Station

MOS Burger was founded in Japan in 1972. It offers a Japanese interpretation of the hamburger, with carefully prepared ingredients and distinctive choices such as rice burgers. For many international visitors, the name itself may be new.

Location: South Building, 1F, Keihan Kuzuha Station Building

There is no wrong meal in Japan

You do not have to eat sushi, ramen or traditional Japanese food at every meal to experience Japan. Sometimes you may want an easy, familiar meal. Sometimes you may simply feel like eating a hamburger or fried chicken. That is completely fine.

Japan is not found only in temples, historic streets and traditional restaurants. It is also present in ordinary places—in the service, small details and everyday habits that local people barely notice.

That, too, is part of travelling.
