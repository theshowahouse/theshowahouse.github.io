Why Is Hirakata a Good Base for Kyoto and Osaka?

A quieter base for exploring Kyoto, Osaka, and Nara

When planning a trip to Japan, many travelers wonder whether they should stay in Kyoto or Osaka.

Kyoto is known for its historic temples and beautiful streets, while Osaka is famous for its food, shopping, and lively atmosphere.

But many visitors have never heard of Hirakata, a city located between the two.

Hirakata offers easy access to both Kyoto and Osaka, while also giving travelers the chance to experience a quieter, more everyday side of Japan.

Kyoto and Osaka from One Place

From Kuzuha Station, the nearest station to The Showa House, you can reach Kyoto and central Osaka by train in about 30 to 40 minutes.

You can stay in one place and take day trips to Kyoto, Osaka, and Nara without changing hotels during your journey.

Without having to carry your suitcase from one accommodation to another, your trip can feel a little more relaxed.

Everyday Life Outside the Tourist Areas

Hirakata is not a famous tourist destination.

In the morning, children walk to school, local residents ride bicycles to the shops, and small businesses quietly begin their day.

In the evening, the neighborhood becomes calm, without the crowds and noise often found in major tourist areas.

Many of our guests enjoy the peaceful atmosphere of this quiet neighborhood—something that is often difficult to find in busy tourist areas.

A Comfortable Place to Settle In

Although the neighborhood is quiet, most everyday essentials are within walking distance.

You will find supermarkets, convenience stores, cafés, restaurants, pharmacies, and KUZUHA MALL, one of the largest shopping centers in the area.

Whether you are staying for three nights or two weeks, it is easy to slow down and enjoy the feeling of everyday life in Japan.

A Different Way to Experience Japan

Visiting the famous sights of Kyoto and Osaka is a wonderful part of traveling in Japan.

But quiet mornings in a residential neighborhood, walking to a local supermarket, and relaxing in a tatami room can also become meaningful parts of the journey.

At The Showa House, we hope you can do more than simply visit Japan.

We hope you can enjoy the feeling of living here, even if it is only for a few days.
