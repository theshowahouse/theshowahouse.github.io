UJI DAY TRIP · JULY 2026

How to Get to Uji by Keihan Railway

Matcha, the Uji River, and an easy local train journey from Hirakata.

When people think of visiting Kyoto, they often picture the famous sights in central Kyoto.

But one of the places we especially recommend is Uji.

Uji is known around the world as one of Japan’s most celebrated matcha destinations.

You can taste fragrant matcha, visit the World Heritage-listed Byodoin Temple, and take a peaceful walk beside the Uji River.

It is a lovely place to spend a slower, quieter day.

From Kuzuha, Uji is about 35 minutes away by Keihan Railway, with one transfer at Chushojima.

Just One Easy Transfer

From Kuzuha Station, take the Keihan Main Line to Chushojima Station.

At Chushojima, transfer to the Keihan Uji Line and continue to Uji.

There is only one transfer, so the journey is simple and easy to understand, even for guests using Japanese trains for the first time.

You also do not need to travel through Kyoto Station, which makes this a comfortable way to reach Uji while avoiding one of the city’s busiest transport hubs.

The Train Ride Is Part of the Journey

The Keihan Uji Line is not a sightseeing train.

It is an ordinary local railway used by people travelling to work, school, and the places they visit in everyday life.

That is exactly what makes the journey enjoyable.

As the train passes residential neighbourhoods and small local stations, you can catch a glimpse of daily life in Japan.

The gentle rhythm of the journey seems to make time move a little more slowly.

I enjoy this part of the trip too.

A Special Railway for Anime Fans

Uji is also well known as the setting of the popular anime series Sound! Euphonium.

On the Keihan Uji Line, you can ride the same familiar green trains that Kumiko and her friends take in the series.

I am a big fan of the series myself.

Those who know the anime may recognise some of the scenery around the railway and Uji Station.

Even without knowing the series, Uji is a beautiful town that is well worth visiting.

A Day in Uji

In Uji, you can:

  • Visit the World Heritage-listed Byodoin Temple
  • Walk beside the Uji River
  • Enjoy matcha sweets and Japanese tea
  • Explore the historic streets
  • Walk a little uphill to a viewpoint overlooking the town

Uji has a calmer atmosphere than central Kyoto.

Rather than rushing from one attraction to another, take your time. Stop at a tea shop that catches your attention, walk beside the river, and enjoy the town at your own pace.

If you would like a longer walk, continue along the river toward Koshoji Temple. Its Kotosaka approach rises gently beneath a canopy of trees and is especially beautiful during the autumn foliage season.

Local Tip

When possible, we recommend visiting Uji in the morning.

It is often quieter earlier in the day, making it a pleasant time to walk beside the river and enjoy the town’s peaceful atmosphere.

The Showa House makes a convenient base for this Uji day trip as well as exploring Kyoto, Osaka, and Nara.

We hope you enjoy a slow and relaxing day there.
